FINAL UPDATE - ACCOUNT APPROVAL REMOVED

- Supervisor, Engineer, and Administrator accounts are created active immediately.
- No administrator approval is required for newly registered accounts.
- Login checks only whether the account is active.
- Removed the user approval checkbox and account-approval count from Admin/User Management.
- Existing legacy `users.approved` database column is retained for schema compatibility and is automatically kept TRUE.
- Maintenance inspection approval/sign-off is unchanged: Engineers and Administrators can still approve or decline maintenance inspections and record electronic signatures.
- Plant assignment and account active/inactive controls remain available to administrators.
